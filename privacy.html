Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

WOW Channels Admin ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the WOW Success and WOW Persian YouTube channels to manage their own channels.

Data we access

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses YouTube data through the YouTube Data API: channel information, videos, and public comments on the owner's channels.

How we use it

The data is used only to read comments, post replies, and moderate comments on the owner's own channels. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Comment text and reply logs may be stored temporarily on the owner's private server for operation. OAuth tokens are stored securely and never shared.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Users can revoke access anytime at Google Account permissions.
